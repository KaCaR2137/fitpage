## Priority Issues

**[P1] Portfolio reads empty on desktop**
- Why it matters: Undermines credibility at the exact section meant to build it — a single small card floating in ~600px+ of dead space (confirmed via screenshot) reads as "not much of a track record" right when a skeptical visitor is evaluating trust.
- Fix: Constrain the section to a centered single-card layout instead of a multi-column grid with one occupant, or reframe the copy to own "first live build" honestly.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] No closing CTA at the natural end of the page**
- Why it matters: Atuty (free consultation, no obligation, 2 revision rounds) is the strongest reassurance block on the page, and it dumps straight into the dark footer with no restated call to action — the visitor's built-up intent has nowhere to land, violating the peak-end rule right at the close.
- Fix: Add a restated CTA block after Atuty, before the footer.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Nav shows 7 simultaneous choices — over the 4-item working-memory threshold**
- Why it matters: `.nav__links` shows 6 text links (O nas, Portfolio, Proces, FAQ, Artykuły, Opinie) plus the CTA pill on every single screen, for a low-web-literacy, mostly-mobile audience — the very first decision point on the page already exceeds the cognitive-load ceiling.
- Fix: Demote secondary links (Opinie, Artykuły) to footer-only, keep 3-4 primary anchors plus the CTA.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`

**[P2] Splash intro replays on every navigation, with no memory**
- Why it matters: ~1.4s delay before real content on every single visit, with no functional payoff for a returning or comparison-shopping visitor.
- Fix: Gate to first visit via `sessionStorage`, or remove.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable optimize`

**[P2] Body copy running ~125 characters/line at desktop width**
- Why it matters: Detector-caught (`p.reveal`), missed by the design-review pass — well past the ~80-char readability guideline, most likely the "O nas" paragraphs or hero subtitle, which is exactly the trust-building copy visitors most need to actually read.
- Fix: Cap the paragraph's measure (a `max-width` in the 60-75ch range) independent of its parent's full width.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable typeset`

**[P3] Two `<a>` elements have line-height 1.20× (below the 1.3× readability floor)**
- Why it matters: Minor, but compounds with tight body copy — worth a quick fix while `/impeccable typeset` is already in scope for the line-length issue above.
- Fix: Bump the affected links' line-height to ≥1.3.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable typeset`
