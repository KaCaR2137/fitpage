## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: The mood is authored; the argument is not. The visual language is genuinely specific and unusually disciplined — one document-length #000 → #f5c518 gradient painted once on body, translucent charcoal glass panels floating on it, the "One Lamp" amber restraint held across every component. That is not the default dark-landing look and it is executed consistently. The hero headline "Niech Twoja forma nie będzie jedyną wizytówką" is pure product character in one line.

But the skeleton is the default freelance-agency template: Hero → About → Portfolio → Contact → FAQ → Footer, every section the same construction (centered 1080px container, glass panel, one title size, fade-up reveal, left-aligned muted text). The gradient is decoupled from meaning — it would look identical behind a law firm. The one genuinely product-specific section, portfolio proof, is unfinished. The "repeatable niche standard" that per PRODUCT.md is the whole reason FitPage isn't a cheap template shop is not expressed in the composition, and the 6-step process is exiled to a separate page.

Deterministic scan (impeccable detect): No design-specificity-class findings — the detector tripped no generic-hero / card-soup / template rule. Quality and slop nits only:
- index.html: 6 — cramped-padding ×4 (.container children reported flush against the panel edge), design-system-radius (2px on a span, already config-acknowledged), em-dash-overuse (8 in body text)
- kontakt.html: 3 — cramped-padding, design-system-radius, dark-glow (amber box-shadow glow on dark page)
- proces.html: 3 — same set as kontakt, including dark-glow
- The dark-glow ignore in .impeccable/config.json is scoped to index.html only, so it suppresses exactly one finding there (the documented phone glow + button-hover lift). The same amber glow trips dark-glow live on kontakt.html and proces.html because the button shadow lives in shared style.css — the ignore should cover those pages or none.

## Overall Impression

The page has a confident, distinctive skin and a genuinely thoughtful robustness layer (works with no JS, every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion, custom controls stay accessible). It also has a hollow centre: a web-design company whose homepage shows no screenshot of a website, an "about" that names nobody, a differentiator that's off-page, and a conversion path that currently dead-ends in placeholder legalese and a "not connected yet" message. The single biggest opportunity: make the portfolio and the process do the persuading the gradient can't.

## What's Working

1. Disciplined, non-template visual system. The one-gradient-on-body + glass-panels construction and the "One Lamp" amber restraint (accent only on the next action, links, focus rings, step numbers, phone glow) is a coherent authored world, executed with rare consistency.
2. The hero earns its keep with copy, not stock art. "Niech Twoja forma nie będzie jedyną wizytówką" delivers the whole pitch with wit and one amber CTA. Not transplantable to another product.
3. Robustness baseline is designed, not box-checked. The .js gate keeps all content visible without JavaScript; reveal / phone-float / consent-pop / "Więcej" all drop under prefers-reduced-motion; the custom consent checkbox keeps a real :focus-visible ring; semantic landmarks, aria-expanded, scroll-margin-top.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The brief form breaks trust at the exact moment of the ask
- What: On kontakt.html, the RODO <details> a cautious visitor opens before typing their phone number reads literally "[Placeholder klauzuli — do uzupełnienia przed publikacją]" with bracketed blanks. script.js answers a completed submit with "To wersja demonstracyjna — wysyłka nie jest jeszcze podłączona." No "what happens next" anywhere on the form.
- Why it matters: The primary conversion action cannot be completed, and the two people most likely to check carefully — a privacy-conscious visitor and a diligent one — both hit a trust-terminal wall. Published as-is, the placeholder clause is also a live legal exposure.
- Fix: Ship the real RODO clause + data-controller identity and wire submission (Formspree / Netlify Forms) before any traffic. If not ready, hide the form and route the CTA to email/phone. Add one line: "Odezwiemy się w ciągu [X] dni roboczych z propozycją zakresu i wyceną."
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

### [P1] Portfolio — the credibility engine — is empty and imageless
- What: One text-only .card (Marta Dominikowska), no screenshot, bare "Zobacz realizację" link. In repeat(auto-fill, minmax(280px, 400px)) it hugs the left of the 1080px panel with ~600px blank beside it on desktop. It's a demo, not a paid client (must not be implied otherwise).
- Why it matters: FitPage sells visual web design to a skeptical non-technical buyer whose first instinct is "show me your work." A paragraph in a half-empty grid reads as "no track record" and "couldn't finish their own site" — the opposite of the "not a generic web shop" positioning.
- Fix: Put a real screenshot / object-fit: cover thumbnail of the Marta demo on the card, labelled honestly as projekt pokazowy. With one item, drop the multi-column grid for a single wide featured layout (image left, text right) so one entry looks deliberate. Consider an annotated "what a FitPage site includes" section-structure visual.
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout

### [P1] Placeholder contact details are live in the markup
- What: mailto:kontakt@fitpage.pl and tel:+48535721592 in the Kontakt section are placeholders per PRODUCT.md / CLAUDE.md.
- Why it matters: If published, direct inquiries vanish silently on a path the page actively promotes.
- Fix: Real address / number before launch, or remove .kontakt__direct and keep only the brief-form CTA until they exist.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

### [P1] Body-copy contrast fails AA in the lower half of the page
- What: Muted #c4c4c4 / light text on the rgba(16,16,16,.70) panels where the black→gold gradient has warmed to #b58f00–#f5c518 behind them (FAQ, contact, approach to footer). Detector medians 3.3–4:1 against the 4.5:1 AA floor for body text; a 390px scan returned 13 low-contrast hits.
- Why it matters: Accessibility failure for low-vision users on the sections carrying the objection-handling and contact content — and the design's signature move (translucent panels over a full-page gradient) is what causes it.
- Fix: Raise panel opacity in the lower sections (or add a subtle solid underlay behind text), or lift body text toward #e6e6e6, or clamp the gradient so the bright zone sits only in the footer margin, not behind any panel. Verify with a contrast checker at the FAQ and footer scroll positions.
- Suggested command: /impeccable audit, then /impeccable polish

### [P2] "O nas" argues the weak case and the real differentiator is hidden
- What: Three paragraphs of category boilerplate, no operator name, no experience, no site count (self-flagged TODO). The 6-step process (Analiza → … → Wdrożenie) — per PRODUCT.md the reason FitPage isn't a template shop — is reachable only via the "Proces" nav word and a footer link, never previewed in the scroll.
- Why it matters: The homepage makes the generic pitch and buries the specific one. A trust-shopping trainer finds abstraction where they expect substance, and the "we"/team voice with zero team info anywhere starts to read as evasive.
- Fix: Lead "O nas" with the checkable standard (fixed trainer-site structure, GDPR checklist from ~20 analysed sites, 6-step process, two revision rounds, delivery on the client's domain with SSL + self-edit guide). Add a compact 6-chip process strip to the homepage linking through to proces.html.
- Suggested command: /impeccable shape

## Minor Observations

- #kontakt section has an <h2>Kontakt but no nav link — scroll-only, discoverable by accident.
- will-change never removed after .widoczna is applied — clear it in the IntersectionObserver callback.
- Portfolio grid math only resolves for exactly 3 items; 1–2 produce asymmetric whitespace.
- On-page H1 carries no query terms ("strony internetowe dla trenerów" is in <title> / meta, not the visible headline) — minor SEO.
- Four identical "Umów konsultację" labels — context-varied microcopy would stop it reading as a loop.
- No color-scheme: dark on a dark-only page — form controls / scrollbars render light-themed.
- Footer over-weights the "Proces" link (prominent .footer__link and a nav item) — makes it look like the page's most important link, which is the brief form.
- em-dash-overuse (detector, advisory) — false positive; the Polish myślnik is correct typography. design-system-radius 2px — already acknowledged in config. Both non-blocking.
- dark-glow on kontakt.html / proces.html — extend the index.html ignore to all three pages (same documented shared-CSS button shadow) or make it a rule-level ignore.

## Questions to Consider

1. A web-design company's homepage with no screenshot of a website — would you hire that designer? What has to be true for the portfolio to carry its own weight?
2. The "we" voice is a deliberate fiction for a solo operator. When the trust-shopping trainer looks for the team and finds only abstraction, does the plural read as confident or as hiding something?
3. Strip the gradient and the amber. What's left that a returning visitor recognises as FitPage and not as a generic dark template?
4. The 6-step process is the whole "we're not a template shop" argument. Why is it the most hidden thing on the site?
5. The signature move — translucent panels on a full-page gradient — is also what pushes body text below AA contrast in the lower sections. Is the gradient worth an accessibility compromise, or can the bright zone live only in the footer margin?
